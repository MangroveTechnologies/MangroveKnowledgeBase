# strategy design

## Summary

Turning a claim about market behaviour into rules an engine can run, and finding out whether the
claim survives contact with data.

## Explanation

Trading styles and the cadence they imply, the archetypes that say why a trade has edge, the trade
plan that writes both down, entry and exit logic, time and regime filters, data quality, and the
backtesting, walk-forward and validation procedures a strategy has to pass before it is believed.

Signals decide what is true of a bar; this subject area decides which signals to compose, in what
role, and under what evidence the composition may be trusted.
