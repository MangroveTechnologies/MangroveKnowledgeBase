# 9. Glossary of Terms

This document provides a comprehensive reference of trading terminology, abbreviations, and definitions used throughout the knowledge base and in professional trading contexts. Terms are sorted alphabetically for easy lookup.

---

| Term | Definition | Abbreviation | Category |
|------|------------|--------------|----------|
| Absorption | When one side absorbs the other's orders without price moving (hidden liquidity) | - | Volume & Order Flow |
| After-Hours | Trading that occurs after regular market hours | AH | Trading Sessions |
| Alpha | Returns in excess of what would be expected given beta exposure | - | Statistics & Quant |
| Analysis Paralysis | Inability to act due to overthinking or excessive analysis | - | Psychology |
| Asian Session | Primary forex trading hours when Tokyo markets are open | - | Trading Sessions |
| Ask / Offer | The lowest price a seller is willing to accept | - | Order & Execution |
| Average Directional Index | Measures trend strength regardless of direction | ADX | Indicators |
| Average True Range | A volatility measure showing the average range of price movement over a period | ATR | Technical Analysis |
| Backwardation | When futures prices are lower than expected spot price | - | Futures |
| Balance | A market state where buying and selling pressure are roughly equal | - | Price Action |
| Beta | A measure of an asset's volatility relative to a benchmark | - | Statistics & Quant |
| Bid | The highest price a buyer is willing to pay | - | Order & Execution |
| Bollinger Bands | Volatility bands placed above and below a moving average | BB | Indicators |
| Break of Structure | When price breaks beyond the most recent swing point in the trend direction | BOS | Technical Analysis |
| Breakout | A price move beyond a defined support or resistance level | - | Price Action |
| Change of Character | When price breaks structure against the prevailing trend; potential reversal signal | CHoCH | Technical Analysis |
| Commodity Channel Index | Momentum oscillator measuring deviation from average price | CCI | Indicators |
| Confirmation Bias | Seeking information that confirms existing beliefs while ignoring contradictory data | - | Psychology |
| Contango | When futures prices are higher than expected spot price | - | Futures |
| Contract Size | The notional value represented by one futures contract | - | Futures |
| Correlation | A measure of how two assets move in relation to each other | - | Statistics & Quant |
| Cumulative Delta | The running total of delta over time | - | Volume & Order Flow |

| FOMO | Fear Of Missing Out; emotional urge to enter trades without proper setup | - | Psychology |

| Keltner Channels | Volatility-based bands using ATR around an EMA | - | Indicators |

| Liquidity | The ease with which an asset can be bought or sold without significantly affecting price | - | Order & Execution |

| Parabolic SAR | Stop and reverse indicator used for trailing stops | PSAR | Indicators |
| Pin Bar | A bar with long wick showing price rejection; reversal signal | - | Chart Patterns |

| Profit and Loss | The realized or unrealized gains or losses on positions | PnL | Risk Management |
| Pullback | A temporary retracement against the prevailing trend | - | Price Action |
| Recency Bias | Overweighting recent events in decision-making | - | Psychology |
| Regular Trading Hours | The main US trading session (typically 9:30 AM - 4:00 PM ET for equities) | RTH | Market Structure |
| Relative Strength Index | Momentum oscillator measuring speed and magnitude of price changes | RSI | Indicators |

| Retest | When price returns to a previously broken level to confirm the break | - | Price Action |
| Revenge Trading | Attempting to recover losses through impulsive, often oversized trades | - | Psychology |

| Rotation | Range-bound price movement oscillating around a central price | - | Price Action |

| Slippage | The difference between expected execution price and actual fill price | - | Order & Execution |
| Sortino Ratio | Like Sharpe but only penalizes downside volatility | - | Statistics & Quant |
| Spread | The difference between the bid and ask prices | - | Order & Execution |
| Standard Deviation | A measure of volatility showing dispersion from the mean | Std Dev / Sigma | Statistics & Quant |
| Stochastic Oscillator | Momentum indicator comparing closing price to price range over time | Stoch | Indicators |
| Stop Loss | A predetermined price level at which a losing position is automatically closed | SL | Risk Management |
| Support | A price level where buying pressure is expected to overcome selling pressure | - | Price Action |

| Tick Size | The minimum allowable price movement for an instrument | - | Order & Execution |
| Tilt | Emotional state where rational decision-making is compromised | - | Psychology |
| Time and Sales | A real-time record of executed trades showing time, price, and size | T&S / L2 | Order & Execution |
| Trend Day | A session dominated by directional movement with minimal pullbacks | - | Price Action |
| True Open | The opening price of the regular trading session | - | Trading Sessions |
| Value Area | The price range containing approximately 70% of session volume | VA | Volume & Order Flow |
| Value Area High / Low | Upper and lower boundaries of the price range containing 70% of session volume | VAH / VAL | Market Structure |
| Value at Risk | The maximum expected loss at a given confidence level | VaR | Statistics & Quant |
| Volume Profile | A display showing volume traded at each price level | - | Volume & Order Flow |
| Volume-Weighted Average Price | The average price weighted by volume; used as a benchmark for execution quality | VWAP | Technical Analysis |
| Win Rate | The percentage of trades that are profitable | - | Risk Management |
| Z-Score | The number of standard deviations a value is from the mean | - | Statistics & Quant |

## Categories

- **Chart Patterns**: Candlestick and bar patterns used to identify potential reversals or continuations
- **Futures**: Terms specific to futures trading including contracts, sessions, and pricing
- **Indicators**: Technical indicators used for analysis and signal generation
- **Market Structure**: Key price levels and session-based reference points
- **Order & Execution**: Terms related to order types, fills, and market mechanics
- **Price Action**: Concepts describing price movement and market behavior
- **Psychology**: Behavioral and emotional aspects of trading
- **Risk Management**: Terms related to position sizing, stops, and capital preservation
- **Statistics & Quant**: Quantitative measures and statistical concepts
- **Technical Analysis**: Chart analysis terms including moving averages and structure
- **Trading Sessions**: Time-based market sessions and related concepts
- **Volume & Order Flow**: Volume-based analysis and order flow concepts
